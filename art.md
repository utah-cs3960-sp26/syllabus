Artwork for CS 3960 Vibe Coding
===============================

All artwork for CS 3960 is generated by Nano Banana Pro, also known as
`gemini-3-pro-image-preview` with the following system prompt:[^1]

[^1]: Though note that the instructors do not plan to re-generate
    artwork if they change the prompt

> The style is Victorian line work, as you'd find in an early printed
> edition of "Alice in Wonderland". Background is solid #EFDEC3. No
> text. Decorative border. Add a dash of whimsy to every illustration
> without breaking genre conventions.

Scene-setting music for each lecture was drawn from the works of
Philip Glass. Illustration ideas and music selection was directed by
GPT 5.2 with the following prompt:

> I am teaching a class on AI-assisted coding, which will cover the
> basics of LLMs & tool-use agents as well as software engineering
> concepts around testing & refactoring. I'll ask for art & music
> ideas; music is played before each lecture for a few minutes, and is
> themed around Philip Glass. The art is used in lecture, and the
> style is Victorian linework as might be present in an Alice in
> Wonderland printing, with whimsical, period-accurate themes. When
> selecting music and art always provide 3–5 suggestions with a
> sentence's commentary on why it is thematically-appropriate.

Instructors would then select from the resulting lists, or in some
cases skip the AI assistance and write illustration prompts or select
music by hand.
