CS 3960 Homework 5
-----------------

Status: draft \
Due: Monday 20 April

In this assignment you will build a self-directed project. Come up
with something cool to show off to the rest of the class! Please do
not create a recipe app or a marketplace app or an app that helps
people exercise or an app that helps people find social activities --
we see enough of those in capstone!

# Requirements

This is a fresh project, start from scratch. Now that you've seen what
AI coding agents can do, you should be appropriately ambitious. Use
whatever coding agent you want! We're agnostic about this.

Push your work [to Github](https://github.com/utah-cs3960-sp26) and
name your repository `final-project-uXXXXXXX`.

# Details and Deliverables

## Week 11 March 30-April 3

By 5pm on Friday April 3, you should have pushed a section called
"Proposal" to the README.md in your repository. It should consist of
two English language paragraphs:
1. What are you going to build? What are the features you intend to show off?
2. What kind of agentic loop are you going to use to make this happen? What will be the feedback mechanisms that you will tell the agent to use, to ensure a quality product?
  
## Week 12 April 6-10

By 5pm on Friday April 10, you should have pushed a section called
"Week 12 Release Notes" to your repo. It should discuss:
- The smartest and dumbest thing your agent loop did this week, and provide links to the relevant AI threads.
- What's working so far on your project? What are your concrete plans for the next week?

## Week 13 April 13-17

By 5pm in Friday April 18, you should have pushed a section called
"Week 13 Release Notes" to your repo. It should:
- Describe how your final product compares and contrasts with your
  proposal from a couple of weeks earlier.
- Links to a 4-5 minute YouTube video describing and showing off your project.
  This should be a public, unlisted video, meaning that anyone with the
  link can watch it. We'd like you to put some effort into production values
  here -- think along the lines of a video that you would be proud to show
  to someone who you're trying to get a job with. The video should:
  1. Describe the problem your project solves.
  2. Demonstrate how it works.
  3. Show 2-3 cool things about your code: show parts of the actual
     implementation (which of course you must understand).
  4. Explain how the agentic loop that you proposed worked out for you.
